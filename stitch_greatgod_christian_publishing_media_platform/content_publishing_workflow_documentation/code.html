Content Publishing Workflow

The lifecycle of editorial content within the GreatGod CMS. This guide outlines the state transitions and required stewardship roles ensuring all publications meet classical standards of rigor.

Stewardship Policies

person_edit

Authors / Contributors

  • Cannot initiate final publication.
  • Cannot approve their own submitted work.
  • Responsible for responding to change requests.
admin_panel_settings

Editors

  • Hold exclusive approval and publishing authority.
  • Must provide written rationale for 'Changes Requested'.
  • Responsible for oversight of scheduled drops.
