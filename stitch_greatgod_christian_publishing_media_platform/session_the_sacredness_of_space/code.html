About this Session

In this profound exploration of Psalm 84, Dr. Chalmers unpacks the biblical theology of sacred space. Tracing the trajectory from the Tabernacle to the incarnate Christ, we discover how the longing for God's presence transforms our understanding of worship, architecture, and the human heart as a dwelling place for the Divine.
