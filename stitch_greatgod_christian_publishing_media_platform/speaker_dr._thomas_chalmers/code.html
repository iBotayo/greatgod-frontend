Dr. Thomas Chalmers

Director of Theological Stewardship

Dr. Thomas Chalmers has dedicated over three decades to the intersection of pastoral care and institutional stewardship. Formerly the Chair of Systematic Theology at St. Jude's College, his work emphasizes the theological foundations of resource management, community support, and the ethical implications of wealth within a faith context. His recent initiatives focus on digital archives as modern sacred texts.
