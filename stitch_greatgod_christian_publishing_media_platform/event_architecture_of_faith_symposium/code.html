Symposium Overview

The Architecture of Faith Symposium gathers leading theologians, architectural historians, and practitioners to explore the profound intersection of physical space and spiritual life. Over three days, we will examine how sacred structures have historically shaped, and been shaped by, theological conviction.

This gathering aims to recover a robust theology of place, challenging modern utilitarian approaches to church design. Through plenary sessions, interactive workshops, and focused theological reflection, attendees will develop a deeper appreciation for the stewardship of sacred spaces in contemporary worship.
