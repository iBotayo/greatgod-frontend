Writer Guidelines

We seek to cultivate a scholarly yet deeply accessible atmosphere. Our writing should illuminate rather than obscure, inviting reflection and clarity.

record_voice_over

Our Voice

Our tone is Scholarly but Accessible. We aim for the clarity of a well-researched essay combined with the warmth of a thoughtful conversation. Avoid overly academic jargon unless defined immediately. The goal is to edify the reader, providing deep insights without creating barriers to understanding.

subject

Formatting

Clean structure is paramount. Use clear headings, short paragraphs, and bullet points where appropriate. Generous whitespace helps the reader pause and reflect on the content.

fingerprint

Originality

Integrity in work is our foundation. All submissions must be original. Proper attribution must be given for all quotes, ideas, and theological frameworks sourced from other authors.
