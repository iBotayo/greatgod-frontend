The Sovereignty of Hope

person Pastor Samuel Okoro
|
calendar_today October 15, 2023
|
menu_book Romans 8:28

In this profound message, Pastor Samuel Okoro explores the tension between present suffering and eternal promise. Drawing from Romans 8, we examine how divine providence weaves through our most challenging seasons, offering an anchor of hope that is not based on wishful thinking, but on the unshakeable character of God.

"And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose." This promise does not negate pain, but it guarantees purpose. Join us as we uncover the sovereignty of hope.
