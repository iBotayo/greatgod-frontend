The Architecture of Faith

by Grace Adeyemi

In this profound exploration, Grace Adeyemi examines the physical and conceptual structures that house human devotion. Bridging historical theology with modern structural philosophy, *The Architecture of Faith* offers a contemplative journey through sanctuaries, altars, and the quiet spaces we construct internally to comprehend the divine.
