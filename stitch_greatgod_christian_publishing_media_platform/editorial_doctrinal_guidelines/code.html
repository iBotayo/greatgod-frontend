Editorial & Doctrinal Guidelines

Our commitment to Scripture First and Theological Accuracy forms the foundation of our stewardship.

menu_book

Scripture First

We hold that the Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God. All content, appeals, and stewardship practices must remain subservient to scriptural truth.

"All Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness."
balance

Theological Accuracy

Our communication avoids sensationalism and manipulation. We strive for precision in doctrine, ensuring that our requests for partnership reflect a sound understanding of grace, provision, and divine sovereignty.

  • Appeals must emphasize God's provision over human urgency.
  • Testimonies should center on Christ's work, not merely human effort.
  • Financial transparency is an act of worship and accountability.
front_hand

Gracious Posture

We approach our donors as ministry partners, not merely funding sources. Our tone is always professional, authoritative in truth, yet characterized by humility and profound gratitude.
